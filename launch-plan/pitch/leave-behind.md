## The problem

Aesthetics is a course of treatments, not a transaction: consultation, consent, treatment, aftercare, review, the next
session. Today that journey lives in paper forms, WhatsApp threads and people's heads. Clinics lose patients between
visits, and when something goes wrong nobody can show who signed off what. Regulation is arriving: England's planned
licensing scheme puts injectables in an "amber" tier with named, supervised practitioners and proper records.

## The product

One platform, two portals, working today across every clinic role.

- **Clinic:** diary, patient records, consent-gated visits (no treatment without a signed, witnessed, locked consent),
  a three-page treatment form that writes the whole record in one step, role-shaped dashboards, practitioner earnings.
- **Patient:** a live roadmap of their plan, journal, skincare routine with adherence, recovery check-ins, before/after
  progress, and a care assistant that answers from their own plan and hands symptoms to a person.
- **Retention:** at-risk scoring, recall tasks and stage-based offers on email, SMS and the portal, with UK marketing
  consent enforced in code.
