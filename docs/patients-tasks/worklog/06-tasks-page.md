### pt-p6-04-bulk-dnd

- `BulkBar` (managers, once rows are selected): "N selected · Tap a teammate on the right to assign, or [Mark handled] … Clear". Mark handled → `completeTasks`.
- Rows are `draggable` for managers; `dragstart` writes the task id to `dataTransfer` and marks the row; Team rows accept `dragover` / `drop` with a hover ring (`0 0 0 2px accent-deep`). The drop reads `dataTransfer` as well as React state, so a drop that lands before React saw the drag start still assigns (found by the probe: synthetic DnD assigned nothing until this). Keyboard / touch alternative: select rows, then tap a teammate.

### pt-p6-05-right-rails

- `TeamPanel` (owner / manager): each member with lane avatar, name, role, open count, "N overdue" in destructive ink and a load bar (open accent-deep, overdue destructive, scaled to the busiest); rows are drop targets; a click filters the list to that person (`?person=`) or, with a selection, assigns it; the hint line changes during a drag or a selection; below, "In front desk pool" and "Unassigned" (pink) tiles.
- `YourDayPanel` (practitioner): Clinical questions (pink, "Reply within 4 hours of the message"), Assigned to you, Your patients, with others (read-only), each a shortcut to its view, and the tip about No answer.
- `TodaysCallsPanel` (front desk): progress bar (success) "x of y handled", the 2×2 grid (In your queue, Waiting in pool, Booked today, Retries scheduled) and the escalation tip.

### pt-p6-06-optimistic-undo-toast

- `src/components/tasks/use-task-actions.ts` (new): `assign`, `handOff`, `claim`, `attempt`, `complete`, `completeMany`, `escalate`, `snooze`. Each patches every cached `["tasks", …]` list at once, calls the server, then shows a sonner toast (5 s) with **Undo** that replays `undoTaskEvent` for the returned event ids (newest first) and invalidates every task query; on error the cache rolls back and the error is the toast. Names ending in "." no longer produce "Sofia M..".
- `useTasksLiveSync()` on the page (realtime in production, 5 s polling in the demo).
