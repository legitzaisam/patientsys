# Phase 8: the Treatments tab

Branch `e2e_exp`, on top of `5cfafe3`. 2 Oct 2026. Four new components under `src/components/patients/record/` replace `TreatmentPlanCard` and the old Treatment history card in `src/routes/_authenticated/patients.$id.tsx`; `src/components/patients/treatment-plan-card.tsx` is deleted (the route was its only importer; the Overview's `SkinPlanSummaryCard` and this tab's roadmap are its two halves now). Layout from `Claude outputs/export/mockup-screenshots/03-…` and `04-…`.

### pr-p8-01-roadmap

- `skin-plan-roadmap.tsx`: `SkinPlanRoadmap({ detail, risk, selectedId, onSelect })`, a `glass-card` with `id="plan"` and `data-qc="treatment-plan-card"` so `#plan` and the journey-board specs still land here. Header "Skin plan roadmap", "<plan> · <clinician> · same view as the patient's Timeline"; right: "n of m milestones" (`data-qc="plan-progress"`, the figure `e2e/patients.spec.ts` compares with the board tooltip and the portal) over a 110px success meter.
- Months from `detail.roadmap` (the portal's `roadmapFor`): a gold-wash header with a 22px number badge (success when every step is done, outlined otherwise), "Month N" in bold and the month title; the steps always visible beneath, indented 16px, never collapsed.
- Step rows are buttons: title, `stepDateLine` ("Completed 2 Sep" / "Booked Sun 4 Oct · 11:30" / "Due 4 Oct" / "No date yet"), then chips: **Not booked** (pink, `stepNeedsBooking`) or **No show** when the journey board says the current step was missed, and the status chip from `stepChip` (Completed / In progress / Upcoming / Skipped). The current step sits on the accent wash with a gold hairline; the selected one carries a 2px accent ring; `aria-pressed` tracks selection.
- The current row is `data-qc="plan-step"` (other rows `roadmap-step`, all with `data-step` / `data-status`), and under its date line it repeats the board's booking note (`data-qc="plan-booking-note"`): `noShowLine` ("Did not attend 24 Sep") or `bookingMismatchLine` ("21 Oct booking is for Microneedling with PRP, not this step"). That reading comes from `listTreatmentPlans` (`["treatment-plans", "patient", id]`, typed as `PlanRiskRow`), the same row the dashboard and the journey board use, so the three never disagree about whether the step is booked.
- `stepDateLine` lives in `src/lib/patients/record-overview.ts` with the other step helpers (`react-refresh/only-export-components` keeps helpers out of component files); one unit test added, 18 pass.

### pr-p8-02-step-details

- `step-details.tsx`: `StepDetails({ step, risk, patientId, canTick, canBook, canEdit, onBook, onEdit })` (`data-qc="step-details"`, `data-step`). Header "Step details" with the status chip (No show in pink when the board says so); the step title and its portal detail text.
- Two tiles: **Due date** (`longDate`, "No due date") and **Booked for** (`data-qc="step-booked-for"`, `data-booked`): the booking's day and time, "Missed 24 Sep" after a no-show, or **Not booked** on a pink tile when a session step has no diary slot (neutral tile for a review or task step). A finished step shows **Completed** with its date instead.
- **Patient checklist** box with "n of m": each item's 14px square (success ✓ when done; gold outline for a clinic-owned item; grey for the patient's own), the label, and the attribution: "patient · 30 Sep" / "clinic · 2 Oct" from `doneByKind` and `doneAt`, "patient" for an open patient item, **You can tick** (gold) for an open clinic-owned item. Clinic-owned items are buttons (`data-qc="step-checklist-tick"`, aria "Tick/Untick …") calling `setMilestoneChecklistItem` and invalidating `["patient-plan", id]` and the patient; the patient's items are read-only here. Empty state "No checklist for this step."
- **Book this step** (`data-qc="plan-book"`, accent pill; shown while the step is open, unbooked and the reader has `appointments.edit`) opens the record's shared `QuickAddAppointment` with `milestoneId` set, and **Edit step** (`step-edit`, white pill) opens the dialog below; the note "Changes here show on the patient's Timeline straight away." closes the card.
- `edit-step-dialog.tsx`: `EditStepDialog({ step, patientId, onClose })`, keyed on the step: Title (required, ≤200), Due date, Status (Upcoming / In progress / Completed / Skipped) and Detail (≤2000) → `updatePlanMilestoneDetails`; success toasts "Step updated. The patient's Timeline shows the change." and invalidates `["patient-plan", id]`, `["treatment-plans"]` and `["patient", id]`, so the roadmap, the Overview card, the journey board and the portal all move together.
- Route: `selectedStepId` state (null means the current step), `editStep` state, `planRows` query; the `bookingStep` QuickAdd moved out of the Overview tab to sit beside `TreatmentFormDialog` so both tabs share it, and now also invalidates `["treatment-plans"]`.

- Rows (`<li data-qc="history-row">`) on the mockup's `96px minmax(0,1fr) 260px` grid at `lg`, two columns at `sm`, one below: date in bold and the clinician (`profiles.full_name`) | treatment name with **View record** (`data-qc="view-treatment-record"`, unchanged hook) on the same line, `treatmentDetailLine` ("Full face · Autologous PRP · 1.5mm depth · £295 · recall 26/12/2026"), the note in an accent-wash box or "No notes" | chips from `treatmentFormChips`: ✓ Treatment record, ✓ Consent, ✓ Aftercare sent, N photos (sky), **Depth not recorded** (pink). `historyFilter` drives the pills; an empty filter reads "No treatments match this filter."
