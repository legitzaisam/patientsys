The journey

Five moments. One golden thread.

Follow one patient from the first booking to the return visit. The screens are drawn from the product with demo data; a few pieces shown are still being built.

0102030405 / 05

  1. ClinicBook

    Booked, and already known.

    Every appointment lands in one diary across practitioners. The front desk panel shows who is waiting, who is in treatment, who is still due, and what to collect today.

  2. ClinicTreat

    One record, in step with the patient.

    The patient record shows where they are in their course, what they have asked, how they are healing and which recall tasks are due, synced to their portal. No treatment starts without a signed consent.

  3. PatientAftercare

    Care that follows them home.

    A note from their practitioner, a recovery check-in shared with the clinic, what to avoid before the next visit, a routine with reminders, and an assistant that answers from their own plan.

  4. PatientProgress

    A roadmap they can see.

    Month by month, the plan shows what is done and what comes next, with how to prepare. If life gets in the way, patients can ask to pause and tell their practitioner why.

  5. BothReturn

    A reason to come back.

    The clinic sets an offer once, for the right stage. It reaches opted-in patients on email, text and their portal, and they book with one tap.
