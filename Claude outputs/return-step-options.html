Return step: options

Your first sentence stays the same in every option. Pick one second sentence and one picture, and I'll put them on the site. The pictures only use features Aetheria already has, and the offers picture now shows the Offers page, not Retention.

The paragraph

Always starts: “Design your own offer templates and switch one on for each stage of the journey, and they send themselves.”

1 · Treatment due dates

…and they send themselves. Every treatment carries its own recall interval, so the clinic always knows who is due back, and when.

From the recall interval set on each treatment in Settings (for example, 120 days for anti-wrinkle).
2 · Offers in the patient's hands

…and they send themselves. Patients find their offer waiting in their own portal and can book with it in a tap.

From the portal's offers strip and the “book using my offer” message.
3 · Recall tasks with an owner

…and they send themselves. Patients who start to drift become recall tasks for a named team member, with a call, text or email one click away.

From recall tasks on the dashboard and patient list.
4 · Who comes back

…and they send themselves. Month-by-month cohorts show how many new patients return for a second and third visit, by treatment and by practitioner.

From “New patient cohorts” on the Retention page.

The picture

Each would sit in the device frame on the right of the Return step.
